How to start

  1. On the TV: Apps → scroll down to App Settings → press 12345 → turn Developer mode on and enter your phone's IP (TV Installer shows it).
  2. Restart the TV (hold the power button ~2 s). After the restart App Settings shows DEVELOP MODE.
  3. Install TvInstaller.apk on your phone, pick the TV, sign in to Samsung once, choose an app.
